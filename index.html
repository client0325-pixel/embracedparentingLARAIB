The Shift

You are not failing. Your nervous system is asking for support.

Laraib’s own path into this work began with postpartum overwhelm and the feeling of trying to hold everything together while quietly losing herself. That lived experience shapes the way she coaches — beginning with the body, the nervous system, and the emotional reality beneath the behavior.

The EMBRACED Framework

A grounded, compassionate approach to parenting support

The old website’s framework remains central to the work — translated into a more modern, accessible experience that highlights the heart of the coaching: connection, calm, and confidence.

Empathy

Seeing the child clearly

We begin by understanding the emotions beneath the behavior, so responses feel less reactive and more attuned.

Mindful Presence

Calm in the moment

Parents learn how regulation supports better communication, less escalation, and more steadiness across the day.

Bonding & Belonging

Connection before correction

Support is designed to strengthen security, trust, and belonging — especially when parenting has felt strained or overwhelming.

Respect

Honoring each family story

Every family is met with care, curiosity, and respect — including cultural, faith, and lived-experience differences.

Attunement

Listening beneath the surface

We slow down and identify what a child may be communicating through their behavior, so responses become more intentional.

Compassion

Gentle, nonjudgmental support

The process is grounded in compassion for both the child and the parent, especially in moments of stress and doubt.

Empowerment

Confidence that grows

Parents leave with more clarity, stronger boundaries, and tools that feel practical rather than performative.

Development

Growth over perfection

The work supports long-term family growth through insight, consistency, and habits that can actually be lived.

About Laraib

A parent coach with lived experience, professional care, and a calm, thoughtful approach

Laraib is a Certified Parent Coach, mother of two, and former chemical engineer who brings a thoughtful blend of structure, empathy, and lived experience to her work. Her path into coaching grew out of her own experience with postpartum overwhelm, which shaped her approach to helping other parents feel supported rather than alone.

She has training in trauma-informed care and foster parenting, and she brings a culturally sensitive, faith-aware lens to her work. Her goal is to help parents build more connected homes, understand their children more deeply, and feel more confident in the way they show up.

“Parenting is not about perfection. It is about presence, connection, and learning how to meet your family with more calm and care.”
Certified Parent Coach through The Jai Institute for Parenting.
Additional training in trauma-informed care and foster parenting.
